How to upload your LinkedIn PDF

  1. Open your LinkedIn profile while logged in.
  2. Click “Resources” (or the “Resources” button) near your profile headline.
  3. Choose “Save to PDF”. LinkedIn will generate and download a PDF of your profile.
  4. Return here and drag & drop or select that PDF file in the uploader on the left.
  5. Optionally add target role, experience level, and dream companies so the AI can tailor scoring, rewrites, and benchmarks.
  6. Click “Analyse & Score” and wait for your review.
